# RUN-003 — probe: charter wording and provenance

**Date:** 2026-10-29, 19:01–19:05 (Asia/Taipei)
**Label:** subagent-embodied probe. Not a full cycle: the Observer and Warden were deliberately not used,
and their work items were abandoned through the new `recover --work-id`.

Two questions, from run 002's findings: does the generator still call keyed programs keyless, and does the
adversary use the declared tool path?

## Answers

**1. Wording fixed.** With the rewritten charter, the generator described its construction as keyed and
said the engine substitutes a secret key for the placeholder:

> "The engine substitutes the same secret byte K at all three *_key positions and ignores the placeholder 0"

No "keyless" claim anywhere, against three for three in run 002. Same program shape (`xor_key` interleaved
with rotations and adds), so the change is attributable to the wording.

**2. Provenance is variable, not systematically broken.** The adversary called `compute`, received
`{"plaintext":"ab8bf711…","key":176}`, and submitted it: verified, one candy, challenge 1 BREACHED. The tool
trace exists this time.

So run 002's untraced solve was a choice, not a property of every session. That is *worse* for evidence than
a constant behaviour: sometimes the trace proves how a breach was reached, sometimes it is absent and the
verifier cannot tell a legitimate offline solve from access to the secret. Any claim built on a breach has
to say which case it is.

## Mechanics confirmed

- `recover --work-id` abandons exactly one dispatch; four targeted calls abandoned four items and left the
  adversary's in place.
- Ticks: generator 1, integrator 1, adversary 2, observer 0, warden 0.

## Still open

The provenance gap itself: the adversary can derive the target outside the declared tools, and the verifier
cannot distinguish that from a leak. Closing it means constraining what a session may do (no local
computation), or recording provenance as unknown rather than implied. The gate is not the weak part; the
evidence bundle is.
