## The hypothesis is worth noting on its own

The Adversary attacked the *harness*, not the cipher: it noticed the published known plaintext is
`bytes(range(4))` and inferred the 24-byte target would be the counter `00..17`. That is a legitimate
metadata attack on the challenge as presented — and it was correctly falsified, because the engine draws the
target from `secrets.token_bytes`. The engine is not leaking; the guess was reasonable and cheap to kill.

Worth recording as a hazard: if the fixture ever used a counter plaintext, this attack would land, and it
would land without touching the construction. The engine's random target is a real defence, not a detail.
