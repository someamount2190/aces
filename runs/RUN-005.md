## The gate held

The adversary called `compute` (turn 1), received the solution, and submitted it (turn 2). Verification
succeeded because the derivation was on record; `breach_confirmed` carries `method: tool_compute`. This is
the behaviour the gate was built to require, and it is now the only behaviour that pays.

## What the run exposed

1. **The catalog is the binding constraint on evolution.** After the first breach the Integrator noted,
   correctly, that a successor must use a *different* candidate, and that with one candidate it would have
   idled. The generator had to produce candidate 2 before escalation was possible. Evolution is therefore
   gated on generation rate, not on attack success — a real dynamic worth measuring in later runs.
2. **My own harness bug charged the observer a turn.** Its turn-1 answer was written by the shell with a BOM,
   so `deliver` recorded `SchemaViolation` against an answer that was actually `{"idle":true}`. The event
   trail shows it (`work_finished`, `error_type: SchemaViolation`, event 3); the agent was not at fault. The
   fix is in the harness, not the charter: answers must be written without a BOM.
3. **Deliberate idling costs events, not turns.** Generator and Observer were dispatched on most ticks and
   abandoned explicitly (`reason: explicit`) when they had nothing to add. Those abandonments are visible in
   the ledger, which is the point: the record says who was asked and who was not.

## What this establishes

A complete supervised cycle is reproducible with real agents, a verifier that pays only for tool-derived
answers, an Observer that records evidence-linked hypotheses, and a Warden that audits the record and finds
nothing crossing a boundary.

## What it does not establish

Unchanged: no autonomy (a turn per tick), no independence (five contexts, one model), no isolation, and
nothing cryptographic. Two toy ciphers were broken by a 256-key search; both the generator and the observer
said so before anyone asked.

The provenance gate proves *how* this breach was reached, not that no session ever looked at something it
should not have. That residue is covered by withholding and audit, and is stated as such in
`ARCHITECTURE.md` §8.
