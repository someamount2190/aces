## What the experiment establishes

**The process improvement works.** Compare run 008, where the same role spent fifteen minutes writing and
compiling C brute-forcers and produced nothing recordable. Here it inspected first, stated a weakness and
the evidence that would confirm it, ran the cheapest test, and recorded the refutation with a reason. No
fourth-wall search, no local tooling, no enumeration.

**The gate and the bench agree.** `solve` refused to run `sequential_recovery` because `causal` was false,
and the Adversary's own analysis had already told it that. The refutation cites the analysis rather than a
vague failure, and the hypothesis stays in its projection so the dead end is not repeated.

**A weak hypothesis is cheap to kill.** Its prediction was wrong in a specific, checkable way — 4096
evaluations of a strategy that cannot apply — and it cost one tool call to refute.

## What it does not establish

- **No attack was found.** The construction survived; that says nothing about its strength, only that the two
  implemented strategies do not apply. The Adversary never spent its oracle budget (0 of 64 queries), so the
  differential and chosen-plaintext tools were not exercised at all.
- **The bench is thin.** Two strategies, both structural; the ladder in `tools/bench.py` shows where they
  stop (diffusion + non-linearity survives, and a semantically-identity rotation is a false negative).
- **One run is not a success rate.** The ladder is the instrument for that; a single cycle is an anecdote.

## Follow-ups this run suggests

1. The Adversary did not reach for `oracle`/`differential` after its structural hypothesis died. The charter
   lists the order but not a fallback: after a refuted structural hypothesis it should open a
   chosen-plaintext hypothesis, since that is the one class of attack the construction's own limitations text
   admits.
2. `solve` returning `strategy: null` is information, but the Adversary could also be told *why* — which
   precondition failed. It inferred correctly here; a weaker session might not.
